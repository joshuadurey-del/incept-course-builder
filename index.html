01 / Get started

One command. Setup in Terminal.

Run this on your Mac and sign in to GitHub. Choose a new course design, a new test course, or one of your saved Builder courses. The test menu handles service connections and requests approval before paid work.

curl -fsSL https://joshuadurey-del.github.io/incept-course-builder/install.sh | bash

02 / Choose your starting point

Your build, from its first decision.

Read the build contract →

Start a new course build saves your audience, standards, goals and design work order. It pauses at design; it does not publish a course.

Create a new test course runs the small native pipeline: one lesson with an article, video and five article checks, plus placement, mastery, unit and final assessments, including a graded written response. It tests the workflow, not a full curriculum.

Resume a course built here reopens your saved design or test. Existing TimeBack courses cannot be imported or overwritten through this flow.
