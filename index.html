Alpha’s course factory, on your Mac

From course idea to finished course.

One typed line. A few hours later a course is live on TimeBack, at the full Incept bar. The build is a 1995-style make: every step a target file, a check and a recipe. No agent, no model in the loop; a model is called only on a work order, as a compiler, and a script decides.

01 / Get started

One command. Setup in Terminal.

Run this on your Mac. Sign in, pick the course from a numbered menu, type three hidden TimeBack strings, and the build runs. Run the same command again to continue; updates keep your workspace and credentials.

curl -fsSL https://joshuadurey-del.github.io/incept-course-builder/install.sh | bash
